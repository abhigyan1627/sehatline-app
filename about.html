About SehatLine

SehatLine is a healthcare technology platform designed to make doctor discovery, appointment booking and clinic queue management simpler for patients, doctors and healthcare teams.

Our Mission

Our mission is to reduce waiting time, simplify access to healthcare and help clinics manage appointments and live patient queues digitally.

What SehatLine Offers

Patients can discover doctors, book appointments and track clinic queues. Doctors and clinics can manage appointments, patient flow and receptionist operations from one connected platform.

Built for Patients, Doctors and Clinics

SehatLine is being developed with a focus on simple healthcare access, practical clinic workflows and digital tools that can work for both cities and smaller communities.
