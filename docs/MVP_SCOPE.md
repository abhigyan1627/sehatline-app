# SehatLine MVP Scope

## Included in this build

- Patient discovery: doctors, PathLabs, filters and side-by-side comparisons.
- Doctor and lab detail experiences with seeded launch-city partners.
- Appointment creation, history, notification and live-token experiences.
- Sehat AI doctor/lab recommendations with budget, distance, gender,
  specialty/test and availability-aware ranking.
- Doctor operations: appointment decisions, OPD queue controls and profile.
- Admin operations: partner verification, bookings, users, notifications and
  launch analytics.
- A local API with seeded persistence and cross-surface demo workflows.
- Responsive light/dark Neo Healthcare design.

## Integration-ready, but requiring credentials

- Firebase phone OTP and Google sign-in.
- Firebase Cloud Messaging.
- Google Maps and live geolocation distance.
- OpenAI-powered natural language intent parsing.
- Cloud storage for reports and prescriptions.
- Razorpay/UPI payments.
- WhatsApp notifications.

## Explicitly deferred from the first launch

- Video consultation, medicine delivery, ambulances and insurance.
- AI diagnosis, medicine prescription and emergency-care replacement.
- Wearable data, health scores and complex subscriptions.
- Separate consumer Sehat AI and PathLab mobile apps.
